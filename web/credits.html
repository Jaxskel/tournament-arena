Integration status

This demo does not log into Tournament, settle money, or award platform points. Tournament’s private ticket validation, scoring, reward and anti-cheat services must be connected and verified before competitive rewards can be enabled. Browser gameplay is backed by an authoritative native server; this is not a claim of complete anti-cheat parity.
