    Game mode

    Demo scoring only. Tournament accounts and payouts are not connected.

    PPK weapon values

    Weapon points follow the Doom tiers, adapted to Quake. Extra Quake weapon mappings are provisional.

    Dollar values are demo examples, not payouts. — means no cash rate is configured. Practice scores nothing.

    How to play

    WASD to move, mouse to aim, left click to fire, Space to jump. Use 1–9 or the mouse wheel to switch weapons.

    Maps rotate after each match. Escape opens the Tournament menu; Resume game returns you to play. The match keeps running while you change settings.

    Frag Race ranks players by frags and previews the top-three prizes. PPK ranks by weapon points. Six bots join each room; bots do not receive prize previews. Practice scores no points.

    Use a desktop browser and allow mouse capture when prompted.
